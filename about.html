FAQ
Is Trend Food USA free?
Yes, completely free. No sign-up or account required.
How accurate is the place information?
Place data is sourced from the Google Places API. Hours and status can change, so we recommend calling ahead before visiting.
How do I find spots near me?
Click the 📍 button on the map to move to your current location, or type your city, neighborhood, or zip code in the search bar.
Can I suggest a new food trend?
Absolutely! We're always watching what's blowing up. Feel free to reach out if you spot a trend we're missing.
Does this work on mobile?
Yes — fully optimized for smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers.
